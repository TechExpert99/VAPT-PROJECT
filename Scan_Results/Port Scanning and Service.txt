STEP 3 — PORT SCANNING & SERVICE DETECTION

1. Basic TCP Port Scan

Command:
nmap 192.168.128.166

Description:
Performed a basic TCP scan to identify open ports and exposed services on the target system.

--------------------------------------------------

2. SYN Scan

Command:
nmap -sS 192.168.128.166

Description:
Performed a TCP SYN stealth scan to identify open ports efficiently.

--------------------------------------------------

3. Service Version Detection

Command:
nmap -sV 192.168.128.166 -oN metasploitable_service_scan2.txt

Description:
Performed service version detection to identify running services and software versions on the target system.
