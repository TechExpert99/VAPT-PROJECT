STEP 3 — OBSERVATIONS

Performed multiple Nmap scans on the Metasploitable 2 target system.

The scans identified numerous open ports and exposed services, indicating a highly vulnerable environment designed for penetration testing practice.

Detected services included:
- FTP
- SSH
- Telnet
- SMTP
- DNS
- HTTP
- SMB
- MySQL
- PostgreSQL
- VNC
- IRC
- NFS

Service version detection identified outdated software versions such as:
- vsftpd 2.3.4
- Apache httpd 2.2.8
- Samba smbd 3.X
- UnrealIRCd
- ProFTPD 1.3.1

Several services appeared outdated and potentially vulnerable to known exploits.
