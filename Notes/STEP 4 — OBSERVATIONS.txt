STEP 4 — OBSERVATIONS

Performed detailed service enumeration and vulnerability analysis on the target system.

The assessment identified several insecure configurations and exposed services including:

- Anonymous FTP authentication
- Exposed SMB information

The target system demonstrated multiple high-risk vulnerabilities suitable for penetration testing exercises.
